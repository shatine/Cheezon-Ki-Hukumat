## 확인된 사실 (대본 단계에서 원문으로 다시 확정)
- **위치와 높이:** Jammu–Baramulla line. Deck 359 m above the riverbed, the world's highest railway bridge.
  - Main arch span is 467 m or 469 m depending on the source; total length 1,315 m.
- **아치:** two ribs of concrete-filled steel boxes. Cantilevered out from each bank, the halves met at the crown on 5 April 2021.
  - Built with cable cranes; one 127 m pylon at the Srinagar end.
- **설계 조건:** wind 260 km/h (some sources 266), 120-year design life, 63 mm blast-resistant steel.
  - Seismic: Zone IV designed to Zone V (some sources say the site itself is Zone V).
- **비탈:** gradients from 43° to 77°; slope stabilisation designed by IISc. Early access by mule.
- **연혁:** work halted in 2008 over alignment and safety; opened in 2025 (Wikipedia: 6 June 2025).
