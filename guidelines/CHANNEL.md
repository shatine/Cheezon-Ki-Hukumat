# Cheezon Ki Hukumat: channel standards (as confirmed in episode 7, 2026-10-24)

The detailed production rules are in `.claude/skills/cheezon-production/`. This page is a one-page summary.

- **Viewers:** Indian adults. The question comes first: start from a contradiction, inconvenience or risk, give partial answers with principle and evidence, and come back to the first question at the end.
- **Order:**
  1. Settle the opening line.
  2. Make the thumbnail text that same line.
  3. Script, review and freeze it.
  4. Hindi, then TTS and measured timings.
  5. Sentence-level plan with an automatic check.
  6. Images, reviewed strictly for consistency.
  7. Videos.
  8. Edit and review.
  9. English and Hindi upload text.
- **Screen:**
  - 3D principle scenes must be moving video.
  - Only 10–20% of the other scenes are video; the rest are images with effects.
  - Change images every 2–4 s.
  - Use as much of each video's usable range as possible.
  - The 30 s opening is 3–5 long, natural videos.
  - No slow-motion, frozen frames or repeats.
- **People:** Indian adults only, faces small, no re-enactment of real people. No text, logos or number plates inside generated images.
- **Language:**
  - Narration and subtitles: Hindi.
  - Review and conversation: Korean.
  - Upload titles and descriptions: separate English and Hindi files.
- **Delivery:** 1920×1080 / 30 fps. Audio −14 LUFS, peak −1.5 dBTP.
- **Thumbnail:**
  - The opening line in Hindi, large and bold with a black outline.
  - Saturated colours by meaning: yellow (question), red (failure), green (number or solution), white (linking words).
  - The picture contrasts expectation with reality.
- **Clean-up:** once a new result is confirmed, delete the version it replaced.
