## 설명
You walk over them every day. But why is almost every manhole cover a circle?

A square lid can slip diagonally through its own hole. A circle can't — its width is the same in every direction. A round cover also needs no lining up, and one worker can roll a 50 kg lid instead of lifting it.

The answer was never about looks. It was about gravity.
