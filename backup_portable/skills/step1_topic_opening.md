# 1단계: 소재 선정 → 참고 영상 분석 → 도입부 문장(= 썸네일 문구)

**누가:** 클라우드. **결과:** `topic_candidates.md`, `opening_candidates.md`, `refs/*.txt`, `refs_analysis.md`, `HANDOFF_epNN.md` 시작.

## 순서
1. **Topic candidates (3–5):** Indian infrastructure, technology or everyday subjects with a visible contradiction, inconvenience or risk.
   - For each candidate write: the contradiction, the possible "big numbers", the principles you can show on screen, and the views of similar videos.
   - Recommend one, and confirm it with the user.
2. **Reference videos:** find **3 videos with 1M+ views** on the same subject (any language). Record the views, length and style in `opening_candidates.md`.
   - Clean the transcripts into `refs/<videoID>.txt` (remove timestamps).
   - `refs_analysis.md` covers each video's flow (numbered), its hook, its comparisons, how it explains principles, and its strengths.
   - Pick out "this is the core reference for our 3D principle videos".
   - **Use only the order of information, hooks and comparisons. Never copy the wording.** Re-verify every number against original sources at the script stage.
3. **Opening line candidates:** 2–3 central questions that show a contradiction.
   - Bring in big numbers ("359m 위 기차, 강바닥엔 기둥 하나 없다!").
   - The user chooses, or rewrites it. **Use what the user rewrote exactly as given.**
4. **Thumbnail text = opening line (Hindi).** Settle the colours too:
   - yellow = question;
   - red = failure or danger;
   - green = numbers or solutions;
   - white = linking words.
   - The title points to the same question.
5. **The 30 s opening:** 3–5 long natural videos (5–10 s each). Contrast "the impressive sight vs the risk or reality".
6. Create `HANDOFF_epNN.md`. It holds:
   - the confirmed items;
   - the step status table;
   - "다음 클라우드 세션이 할 일";
   - the user's preferences.

## 완료 기준
- The user has confirmed the topic, the opening line and the thumbnail text, and they are recorded in HANDOFF.
